# Proximity carousel speaker reminders

The dedicated **Limits of public reconstructions** slide has been removed. When discussing the episode library, note that reconstructed orbital paths depend on public TLEs, propagation assumptions, and the age and availability of observations. Numerical separation and speed estimates are illustrative, not measured closest approaches.

The dedicated **Future research** slide has been removed. Before the discussion questions, briefly mention opportunities to compare publicly reconstructed behavior with additional observations and to develop shared descriptions of close approaches, shadowing, and other proximity operations.
